## FAQ

**Can I get downloads for one iOS app?**  
No. This connector is the top free and top paid charts.

**Where are Android downloads?**  
On the App Downloads connector, using the Android bundle ID.

**Is the App Store connector free?**  
Yes. The free plan has 100 requests a month and includes every source. No credit card. Paid plans start at $19 a month for 1,000 requests.

**Which AI apps work with it?**  
Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code, Grok, Muse, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Windsurf and any app that supports MCP connectors.
